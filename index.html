What costs you calls, in plain sight

We read your profile the way a customer does. The same six things come up almost every time.

1

Reviews nobody answered

The newest review is the first one people read. If the owner never replied, that is what they remember.

2

No new reviews in months

Google and customers both read a quiet profile as a quiet business, even when your crews are flat out.

3

A description that says nothing

Who you are, where you work, what you are known for. Most profiles have one line or none.

4

Services with no detail

Each service you list can carry a sentence. Almost none do, and Google uses them to decide who to show.

5

Photos from years ago

Fifteen to twenty real job photos, before and after, do more than any ad. Phone pictures are fine.

6

A website link that is broken

We have seen a profile send customers to a page that no longer exists. Nobody at the company knew.
